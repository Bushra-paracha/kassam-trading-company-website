Our company

A family business built for global trade.

Founded in Karachi in 1999 by Muhammad Idrees Piracha, Kassam Trading Company has grown through relationships, product knowledge and a practical understanding of international rice trade.

Our story

From Karachi roots to international supply.

KTC is a family-owned rice milling, processing and export company. We focus on helping buyers source Pakistani rice with agreed specifications, suitable packaging and organized export support.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the buyer’s market, communicate clearly, prepare the product carefully and support the shipment from enquiry to dispatch.

Our mission

To make Pakistani rice sourcing dependable, transparent and commercially useful for international buyers.

Our values

Integrity, consistency, responsiveness and respect for long-term trade relationships.

Our role

Rice millers, processors and exporters serving importers, distributors and food businesses.
